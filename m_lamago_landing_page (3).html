THE PROBLEM

Many visitors experience Hawaiʻi without understanding its culture, history, or values—missing the deeper meaning behind the wonderful places they visit.

🌿

OUR SOLUTION

MālamaGo turns every trip into a welcoming, joyful adventure through location-based challenges, cultural education, and rewards that support local families.

🌊

OUR IMPACT

We inspire respectful tourism, preserve Hawaiian heritage, and give back warmly to the community that makes the islands so magical.

Our Ohana Mission

Care for the land, care for the people.

MālamaGo encourages respectful tourism, preserves Hawaiian culture, supports local businesses, and inspires everyone to become wonderful stewards of the islands.

For Travelers

Experience Hawaiʻi beyond standard guidebooks. Learn with a smile, explore safely, and create unforgettable memories.

For Local Businesses

Connect with kind, engaged travelers, grow your family business, and build strong ties with visitors.

For Hawaiʻi

Preserve island culture, protect nature, and give back lovingly to the places that make Hawaiʻi so special.

Got Questions?

Friendly answers to what you're wondering

1. Is it really worth $29 when there are free travel blogs everywhere?

Free blogs often lead you to overcrowded parking lots with outdated tips. MālamaGo saves you hours of planning stress, guides you respectfully, and unlocks partner discounts that easily pay for the pass on day one!

2. Will this actually work for me if I'm traveling with family or kids?

Yes! Quests are tailored for all paces—ranging from easy historic town walks and family-friendly cultural centers to scenic nature hikes. You choose what fits your ohana best right from your phone.
